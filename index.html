Good to know

QUESTIONS?
WE'VE GOT ANSWERS.

Do I need to provide photos?

Yes. For the $199 founding package, you provide approved photos, menu details, and current promotions. On-site photography is not included.

Can I approve content before it goes live?

Absolutely. You review designs and captions before scheduling. One round of revisions is included each month.

What platforms do you support?

The founding package covers content scheduling for Instagram and Facebook, subject to the access and permissions available on your business accounts.

Is there a long-term contract?

We'll discuss the service terms and any cancellation requirements with you before work begins. Nothing is booked or charged by submitting the inquiry form.

Does the package include ads or photography?

No. Paid advertising, ad spend, daily community management, and on-site photography are outside the $199/month founding package.

Can I request a free sample post?

Yes. Use the inquiry form and choose “Free sample post.” We'll follow up about your business and the materials needed to create a sample.
